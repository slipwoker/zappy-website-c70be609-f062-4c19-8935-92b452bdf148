Our Editorial Values

The principles that guide every story we tell and every forecast we deliver.

Factual Reporting

We are committed to rigorous verification and sourcing. Every story undergoes a multi-layered editorial review to ensure accuracy, fairness, and context before it reaches our community. In an era of misinformation, our promise is truth you can trust.

Neighborhood Presence

WYNC is woven into the fabric of our local communities. Our reporters live here, shop here, and raise families here. We don't just cover the neighborhood — we are the neighborhood, bringing you stories that matter from voices you know.

Timely Communication

When severe weather strikes or breaking news unfolds, seconds count. Our Doppler radar technology and dedicated weather team ensure you receive life-saving alerts and critical updates faster than any other local source, keeping you prepared and protected.
